I'll try the physio in between all of this, I guess.

But you know what's even *more* fun? Menopause. Especially with autism and bipolar disorder.

Okay, sarcasm aside, I got a lot of other curveballs in my life besides the above-mentioned, so I'm happy to announce that mentally? Kinda alright. Kinda doing fairly okay.
